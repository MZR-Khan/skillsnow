How Google Drive Folder Connection Works

SkillsNow Computer Institute links student certificates and Detail Marks Certificates (DMC) securely to cloud storage.

1

Upload Student File

Upload the student's certificate or DMC PDF to your public Google Drive folder.

2

Name File by Student ID

3

Instant Cloud Fetch

When a student enters their ID, the portal directly opens or embeds their specific Google Drive document!
